What this page records

It records the times you press these buttons — when you clock in and out, and when your breaks start and end — and it tells the server this page is open, roughly once a minute.

It cannot see anything outside this page. Your browser does not let a website watch your typing, your other tabs, your other applications or your files, and this page does not try. If you close this tab you are still clocked in; your team leader is not alerted for it.
