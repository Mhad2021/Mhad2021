What this system records

A plain-language description of every piece of data {{ app_name }} collects from your work laptop.

In one line: it records when you are working and whether your computer is being used — not what you are doing on it.

✅ What is collected

  • The time you clock in and clock out
  • Approved breaks you start and end, and how long they lasted
  • Whether your computer has had keyboard or mouse input recently — a number of seconds only
  • Whether your workstation is locked
  • Whether your laptop is reachable: on, asleep, shut down or disconnected
  • Your computer's name, operating system version and the tracker version
  • Your work schedule, so late arrivals and early departures can be identified

🚫 What is never collected

  • Keystroke content. The tracker asks the operating system how long it has been since the last input. It never reads what was typed.
  • Passwords of any kind
  • Messages, emails or chat content
  • Screenshots or screen recordings
  • Webcam or microphone — the tracker does not request access
  • Personal files or documents
  • Browsing history or the websites you visit
  • Which applications you have open
  • Your location

When your team leader is notified

Your computer has no keyboard or mouse input for {{ policy.idle_threshold_seconds | duration }}, and no break is running
A short break runs longer than {{ policy.short_break_max_seconds | duration }} (plus {{ policy.break_overrun_grace_seconds | duration }} grace)
A lunch break runs longer than {{ policy.lunch_break_max_seconds | duration }} (plus {{ policy.break_overrun_grace_seconds | duration }} grace)
Your laptop stops reporting for {{ policy.offline_alert_after_seconds | duration }} while you are still clocked in
Breaks pause the alerts. While an approved lunch or short break is running, no inactivity alert is sent. Use the break buttons in the tracker and you will not be flagged for stepping away.

How it works

The tracker sends a short status message to the server every {{ policy.heartbeat_interval_seconds }} seconds while you are clocked in. That message contains your idle time in seconds, whether the screen is locked, and nothing else about your activity.

If those messages stop arriving for {{ policy.heartbeat_grace_seconds | duration }} — because the laptop was shut down, lost its connection, went to sleep, or the tracker was closed — the server records that your computer went offline and, after {{ policy.offline_alert_after_seconds | duration }}, notifies your team leader.

You can see everything recorded about you on your My day page. If something looks wrong, ask your manager to correct it — corrections are logged with the original value kept.

Your rights

Depending on where you work, you may have a legal right to see the data held about you, to have inaccurate records corrected, and to be told the purpose of the monitoring before it starts.

This page is the notice of that purpose: managing attendance and working hours. If you have questions about how your employer uses it, contact your HR or people team — not the system administrator.
